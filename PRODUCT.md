# Product

REWEAR helps people complement existing outfits with affordable clothing suited to their preferred brands, fit, colors and previous choices. A swipe feed keeps one item, price and explanation in focus. The interface is German.

The seasonal-color estimate runs locally from a camera shot or existing portrait. The optional macOS companion works on a locally exported Apple Photos snapshot, uses Apple Vision body poses and clothing/image features, and keeps ambiguous person/garment assignments available for review. It does not independently identify a person, measure a body or prove garment identity.

Keep all personal images and preferences local. Clearly distinguish dated price references from retrieved retailer data. Explain fit and color recommendations without invented certainty percentages. Preserve existing browser storage when updating the app.
