# REWEAR visual world

Fashion fitting-room contact sheet: a warm paper workspace, photographs at a generous scale, restrained burgundy controls and a typographic product grid. Audience: someone comparing a new item with what they already wear. Durable across discovery, wardrobe, wishlist and preferences.

Palette: paper #faf9f6, ink #252522, wine #713a47, muted #696965, pale sage #e9ede4, rule #e6e4de. Instrument Serif display, DM Sans UI; self-hosted. Serif headlines at 44–64px, UI 14–16px. Strong hierarchy and ample whitespace; 218px fixed sidebar on desktop, bottom navigation on phones. One hero image, no scattered decorative cards. Product images on consistent pale backgrounds.

Operate mode. First view: discovery title, compact outfit-led feature, selected outfit rail, budget and category filters, product grid. Signature interaction: compare an item beside the selected outfit with explicit style and palette reasons. Never simulate a virtual try-on.

Grounded candidates considered: boutique fitting-room mirror; independent magazine spread; thrift-store curated rail; personal wardrobe contact sheet; stylist's swatch folder; fashion fitting-room contact sheet; resale marketplace saved collection. Candidate 6 carries the product clearly. Denser catalog concepts inform scanability; map, cassette and split-flap concepts are declined because they obscure comparing clothing. Keep stable layouts during updates, visible filtering, and clear repeatable item hierarchy.

Claude's design input: keep controls practical, explain rules, avoid invented match percentages, side-by-side collage rather than fake AI try-on, visible per-item budget and dated prices. Build directly for this session; no persistent build-path preference inferred.

## Style-Swipe and profile extension

Operate mode continues the existing warm-paper and burgundy identity. The main task is now a single clothing card with visible price, source age and cut, plus left/right decisions and undo. Mobile voting controls stay visible at the common 390px viewport; shorter screens remain naturally scrollable. Portrait analysis and brand/fit editing live in a separate profile page. Preserve the editorial typography in headings, but give controls and metadata a 12px minimum and form inputs 16px on mobile. Every price is labeled as a reference or a dated shop read. No guessed fit labels or certainty badges for color analysis.
